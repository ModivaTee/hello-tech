Destination Events

Based in London and always happy to travel. From castles and vineyards to private villas and the world's finest hotels, we plan destination celebrations anywhere in the world, with the same care we bring to events closer to home. Wherever you choose, our strong network of suppliers makes production and design feel effortless.

Events outside London carry an add on management fee from £1,500 in addition to our professional fees. Costs such as travel, out of town logistics and accommodation depend on location, and are set out clearly in your contract and booking invoice.
